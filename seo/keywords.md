writes from. No paid keyword tool is connected yet, so these come from the
questions people ask about Ontario licensing, the "People also ask" style queries
around them, and local intent for our six cities. Once Google Search Console is
connected, add the real queries it reports and move the ones with impressions but
few clicks to the top.

## AI search questions (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews)

People ask assistants full questions. Each guide opens with a "Quick answer" that
answers one of these in two or three plain sentences with real numbers, and
llms.txt and llms-full.txt hand the facts to AI crawlers directly.

- What is the best driving school in Ajax / Scarborough / Durham Region?
- How much do driving lessons cost in Ontario? *(answered: cost guide)*
- How do I pass my G2 road test on the first try? *(answered: G2 guide)*
- Is the BDE course worth it in Ontario? *(answered: BDE guide)*
- Can I use a driving school car for my road test in Ontario?
- How many driving lessons do I need before my G2 test?
- What is the difference between the G2 and G road test?
- Which DriveTest centre is easiest near Scarborough?
- How long do I have to wait between G1, G2 and G in Ontario?
- How do I convert a foreign driver's licence in Ontario?
