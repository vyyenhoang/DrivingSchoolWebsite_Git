Whether you just passed your G1 knowledge test or you are getting ready for your G2 or G road test, this guide explains how lessons in Whitby work, which local roads help you prepare, where Whitby drivers usually take their test, and what it all costs.

What does a driving lesson in Whitby look like?

Every lesson is one-on-one, so the full hour is yours. Your instructor picks you up from your home, school or workplace in Whitby and plans the lesson around what you need next.

Your first lesson usually includes:

  • Getting set up: seat, mirrors and steering wheel position before you move.
  • A quick assessment: starts, stops, turns and lane position on quiet streets.
  • Honest feedback: what you already do well and which habits would cost marks on a road test.
  • A plan: a realistic estimate of how many lessons you need and what each one will cover.

Each lesson after that picks up where the last one ended, with extra time on the skills family practice often misses, such as full stops and shoulder checks.

What licence do you need to start lessons in Whitby?

You need a G1 licence, which you get by passing a vision test and the written knowledge test at a DriveTest centre. Once you have it, you can start lessons with a certified driving instructor right away.

While you hold a G1 in Ontario, you must:

  • Keep a zero blood alcohol level.
  • Not drive between midnight and 5 a.m.
  • Not drive on 400-series highways or high-speed expressways such as the 401.
  • Always drive with a fully licensed driver who has at least four years of driving experience.

There is one important exception. When you drive with a driving instructor certified in Ontario, you can drive on any road. For Whitby drivers, that matters: Highway 401 runs along the south end of town and Highway 407 crosses the north, and a lesson is the only way to practise on them while you hold a G1.

Where do Whitby drivers take their road test?

The Oshawa DriveTest centre is the closest option for most Whitby students. Some prefer to book Port Union or Metro East in Scarborough when those centres have earlier openings. Check the current list of centres and available dates on drivetest.ca, since availability changes often.

Choose a centre whose surrounding roads you can practise on before your test. Our guides to Oshawa DriveTest and Metro East DriveTest explain what the areas are like and what to expect when you arrive. If you are not sure which one suits you, we can help you find an open slot.

How many lessons will you need?

Ontario does not set a minimum number of lessons. It does set waiting periods: you must hold your G1 for 12 months before your G2 road test, or 8 months if you finish a government-approved driver education course. After that, you hold your G2 for 12 months before you can take the G road test.

As a rough guide for Whitby students:

  1. Confident drivers who practise often with family usually need 2 to 3 hours of test-focused lessons.
  2. Drivers who still hesitate at left turns, lane changes or busy intersections usually need 4 to 5 hours.
  3. Complete beginners do best with 10 hours or a full BDE course.

Is the BDE course worth it for Whitby drivers?

A government-approved BDE course lets you take your G2 road test after 8 months instead of 12. Many insurers also offer a discount to new drivers who complete one, so ask your insurance company before you decide. Our BDE packages combine online theory with one-on-one in-car lessons in Whitby and the surrounding area. Read whether the BDE course is worth it for a fuller comparison.

Tips for getting the most out of your lessons

  • Practise between lessons. Even 20 minutes on quiet streets helps new skills stick.
  • Ask questions. Your instructor would rather explain a right of way rule twice than watch you guess on test day.
  • Book your last lesson near your test centre. Seeing the area beforehand helps settle your nerves.
  • Know the common mistakes. Read our list of G2 road test automatic fails, and for the G test, our highway tips.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer pick-up in Whitby?

Yes. Your instructor picks you up from your home, school or workplace in Whitby, including Brooklin, and drops you off after the lesson at no extra charge.

Can I practise on Highway 401 with a G1?

Only with a certified driving instructor. G1 drivers cannot use 400-series highways with a family member, but when you drive with an instructor certified in Ontario, you can drive on any road.

Which DriveTest centre is closest to Whitby?

For most Whitby drivers, the Oshawa DriveTest centre is the closest. Port Union and Metro East in Scarborough are other options. Check drivetest.ca for current locations and open dates.

Can I use your car for my road test?

Yes. Every hours plus road test bundle, and the Premium and Advanced BDE packages, include the use of our car on test day along with a warm-up beforehand.

Can I take lessons without signing up for BDE?

Yes. You can book a single lesson or an hours plus road test bundle as soon as you have your G1.
