Whether you just got your G1 or you are getting ready for your G2 or G road test, this guide explains how lessons in Pickering work, which local roads help you prepare, where Pickering drivers usually take their test, and what it costs.

What does a driving lesson in Pickering look like?

Every lesson is one-on-one. Your instructor picks you up from your home, school or workplace in Pickering, and the hour is planned around what you need next, not a fixed script.

A typical first lesson covers:

  • Setting up the car: seat, mirrors and steering wheel before you move.
  • A short assessment: starts, stops, turns and lane position on quiet streets.
  • Clear feedback: what you already do well and the habits that would cost marks on a road test.
  • A plan: a realistic estimate of how many lessons you need and what each one will cover.

After that, each lesson picks up where the last one ended. If you practise with family between lessons, your instructor will spend the time on the skills family practice tends to miss, like proper observation at intersections and smooth lane changes.

What licence do you need to start lessons?

You need a G1 licence. You get it by passing the vision test and the written knowledge test at a DriveTest centre. Once you have it, you can drive with a certified driving instructor right away.

G1 drivers in Ontario must:

  • Keep a zero blood alcohol level.
  • Not drive between midnight and 5 a.m.
  • Not drive on 400-series highways or high-speed expressways, unless the accompanying driver is a driving instructor certified in Ontario.
  • Always drive with a fully licensed driver who has at least four years of driving experience.

The highway rule is a big one in Pickering. Highway 401 runs along the south end of the city and Highway 407 crosses the north, and the only way a G1 driver can practise on them is with a certified instructor.

Where do Pickering drivers take their road test?

There is a choice of nearby centres. Pickering students often book Port Union or Metro East in Scarborough, or Oshawa to the east. Check the current list of centres and open dates on drivetest.ca, since availability changes often.

Pick the centre whose roads you can practise on before your test. Our guides to Metro East DriveTest and Oshawa DriveTest describe the surrounding areas and what to expect when you arrive. If you are unsure which one suits you, we can help you find an available slot.

How many lessons will you need?

Ontario does not set a minimum number of lessons. What it does set is waiting time: you must hold your G1 for 12 months before your G2 road test, or 8 months if you complete a government-approved driver education course.

As a rough guide for Pickering students:

  1. Confident drivers who practise often with family usually need 2 to 3 hours of test-focused lessons.
  2. Drivers who still hesitate at left turns, lane changes or busy intersections usually need 4 to 5 hours.
  3. Complete beginners do best with 10 hours or a full BDE course.

Is the BDE course worth it for Pickering drivers?

A Ministry-approved BDE course lets you take your G2 road test after 8 months instead of 12. Many insurers also offer a discount to new drivers who complete one, so it is worth asking your insurance company before you decide. Our BDE packages combine online theory with one-on-one in-car lessons in Pickering and the surrounding area. Read whether the BDE course is worth it for a fuller comparison.

Tips for getting the most out of your lessons

  • Practise between lessons. Even 20 minutes on quiet streets with a family member helps new skills stick.
  • Ask about anything you are unsure of. Your instructor would rather explain a right of way rule twice than watch you guess on test day.
  • Book your last lesson near your test centre. It lets you see the area and settle your nerves.
  • Read the common mistakes. Our list of G2 road test automatic fails shows what examiners watch most closely.

Do you teach outside Pickering?

Yes. We are based in Ajax, and our instructors also teach in Pickering, Whitby, Oshawa, Scarborough and North York, with free pick-up and drop-off within our service area. Many Pickering students start lessons near home and finish with a lesson around the test centre they booked.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer pick-up in Pickering?

Yes. Your instructor picks you up from your home, school or workplace in Pickering and drops you off after the lesson, at no extra charge.

Can I practise on Highway 401 or 407 with a G1?

Only with a certified driving instructor. G1 drivers cannot use 400-series highways with a family member, but highway practice is allowed during a lesson with an instructor certified in Ontario.

Is there a DriveTest centre in Pickering?

Pickering drivers usually test at a nearby centre such as Port Union, Metro East or Oshawa. Check drivetest.ca for current locations and open dates.

Can I use your car for my road test?

Yes. Every hours plus road test bundle, and the Premium and Advanced BDE packages, include the use of our car on test day along with a warm-up beforehand.

Can I take lessons without signing up for BDE?

Yes. You can book a single lesson or an hours plus road test bundle as soon as you have your G1.
