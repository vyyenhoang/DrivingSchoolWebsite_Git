How Much Do Driving Lessons Cost in Ontario? 2026 Price Guide

By Public Star Driving School · October 30, 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer

At Public Star Driving School, a one-hour G2 lesson costs $50 + HST ($56.50) and a G lesson costs $60 + HST ($67.80). Hours plus road test bundles start at $200 + HST for G2, and the full MTO-approved BDE course starts at $499 + HST. DriveTest charges its own test fees on top: $53.75 for the G2 road test and $91.25 for the G road test.

Driving lessons in Ontario are priced three ways: by the hour, as a bundle of hours plus the use of a car for your road test, or as a full Beginner Driver Education (BDE) course. Which one is cheapest for you depends on where you are in the licensing process. This guide uses our real 2026 prices for Scarborough, North York, Pickering, Ajax, Whitby and Oshawa, so you can see exact totals with HST.

DriveTest fees you pay separately

Driving school prices never include government test fees. You pay these directly to DriveTest when you book:

  • G1 package: $159.75, which covers the knowledge test, your first G2 road test and a five-year licence
  • G2 road test: $53.75 (for a retest, or if you did not buy the G1 package)
  • G road test: $91.25
  • Knowledge test re-attempt: $16

Fees are set by the province and can change, so confirm them on the DriveTest fees page before you pay.

What a lesson price should include

When you compare driving schools, a lower hourly rate does not always mean a lower total. Check these before you book:

  • Pick-up and drop-off. Ours is free anywhere in our service area. Some schools charge extra or ask you to come to them.
  • The car on test day. Using a school car for the road test is often an add-on elsewhere. Our bundles include it.
  • Lesson length. Make sure an "hour" is 60 minutes of driving, not including the drive to your home.
  • HST. Some schools quote prices with tax and some without. We always show both.
  • One-on-one time. Lessons should be just you and the instructor.

How many lessons do you need?

It depends on how much you already drive. As a rough guide:

  1. Brand new drivers usually do best with the BDE course or 10 hours plus road test.
  2. G1 drivers who practise with family often need 3 to 5 hours to fix habits and learn the test route.
  3. Experienced drivers from another country often need 2 to 3 hours to learn Ontario rules and what examiners look for.
  4. G2 drivers going for their G usually need 2 to 4 hours, mostly on highway skills.

Book one lesson first if you are unsure. Your instructor can tell you after an hour how many more you are likely to need.

Frequently asked questions

How much is one driving lesson in Ontario?

At Public Star Driving School, one hour costs $50 + HST ($56.50) for G2 preparation and $60 + HST ($67.80) for G preparation, with free pick-up and drop-off.

Is it cheaper to buy a driving lesson package?

Usually, yes. Our 5 hours plus road test G2 bundle is $330 + HST, which includes our car on test day. Buying 5 single lessons and renting a car for the test separately would cost more. See our guide to renting a car for your road test.

Do driving lesson prices include the road test fee?

No. DriveTest fees are paid to DriveTest. The G2 road test is $53.75 and the G road test is $91.25.

Do I have to pay before booking?

No. You can request a lesson online with no payment. We confirm your time first and you pay by e-transfer or in person before your first lesson.
