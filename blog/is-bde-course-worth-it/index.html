Is the BDE Course Worth It in Ontario? Cost, Insurance and the 8-Month Rule

By Public Star Driving School · October 30, 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answer

For most new drivers, yes. An MTO-approved BDE course lets you take your G2 road test after 8 months with a G1 instead of 12, many insurers offer a discount to BDE graduates, and you get at least 10 hours of professional in-car training. At Public Star, BDE starts at $499 + HST ($563.87).

Beginner Driver Education, or BDE, is the Ministry of Transportation approved course for new drivers in Ontario. It is not required to get your licence, so the real question is whether the time it saves and the training you get are worth the price. Here is how to decide.

What the BDE course includes

Every MTO-approved BDE course follows the same core structure:

  • 20 hours of classroom or online theory, covering rules of the road, defensive driving and hazards
  • 10 hours of one-on-one in-car lessons with a certified instructor
  • 10 hours of homelink assignments, practical tasks you do with a family member or on your own

When you finish, the school submits your certificate to the Ministry.

The three main benefits

1. Your G2 road test 4 months sooner

Without BDE, you must hold your G1 for 12 months before taking the G2 road test. With a BDE certificate, the wait drops to 8 months. If you need to drive for work or school, those 4 months matter.

2. A possible insurance discount

Many Ontario insurers give new drivers a discount for completing an approved course. The amount varies by company, so ask your insurer for a quote with and without a BDE certificate. For young drivers, the yearly saving can make up a large part of the course price.

3. Better habits from day one

Ten hours with a professional instructor builds correct habits before bad ones form. Students who learn only from family often pick up rolling stops or missed shoulder checks, which are exactly what examiners fail people for.

When BDE is worth it, and when it is not

BDE is usually worth it if you:

  • Are a new driver with little or no experience
  • Want to take your G2 test as early as possible
  • Are under 25 and paying high insurance premiums
  • Do not have a family member who can supervise lots of practice

You may not need BDE if you:

  • Have already held your G1 for 12 months
  • Already drive confidently and only need test preparation
  • Are an experienced driver from another country

In those cases, a few hours plus a road test bundle is often the better value.

Frequently asked questions

Is the BDE course mandatory in Ontario?

No. BDE is optional. It lets you take your G2 road test after 8 months instead of 12 and may lower your insurance.

How long does the BDE course take?

The course is 40 hours in total: 20 hours of theory, 10 hours in the car and 10 hours of homelink. Most students finish in a few weeks, depending on how often they book in-car lessons.

How much insurance do I save with a BDE certificate?

It depends on your insurer, age and location. Ask your insurance company for a quote with and without the certificate before you decide.

Can I start BDE before my G1 is 8 months old?

Yes. You can take the course any time after getting your G1. The 8-month wait for your G2 road test is counted from the date you got your G1.
