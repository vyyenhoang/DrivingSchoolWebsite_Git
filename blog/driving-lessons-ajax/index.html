Driving Lessons in Ajax: What to Expect

By Public Star Driving School · November 8, 2026 · 6 min read

If you are a new driver here, or you are getting ready for your G2 or G road test, this guide explains what a lesson actually looks like, which local roads are worth practising on, where Ajax drivers usually take their road test, and what it costs.

What happens in your first driving lesson in Ajax?

Your first lesson is mostly about finding out where you are. Your instructor picks you up from your home, school or workplace and starts on quiet residential streets before moving to busier roads once you are comfortable.

During that first hour, expect to:

  • Adjust the car properly: seat, mirrors and steering wheel, before you move.
  • Show what you can already do: smooth starts and stops, turns, and lane position.
  • Get honest feedback: a short list of what you do well and what needs work.
  • Agree on a plan: how many lessons you likely need and what each one will cover.

If you have never driven before, the first lesson stays in quiet areas. If you already practise with family, your instructor will move quickly to the things that cost marks on a road test.

What do you need before you can book a lesson?

You need at least a G1 licence. Ontario gives you a G1 after you pass the vision test and the written knowledge test at a DriveTest centre. With a G1, you can drive with a certified driving instructor, and lessons are a good way to start safely.

Remember that G1 drivers must:

  • Keep a zero blood alcohol level.
  • Not drive between midnight and 5 a.m.
  • Not drive on 400-series highways or high-speed expressways, unless you are with a driving instructor certified in Ontario.
  • Always drive with a fully licensed driver who has at least four years of driving experience.

That third rule matters in Ajax, because Highway 401 runs right through town. With a certified instructor, you can practise highway merging before you are allowed to do it with family.

Ajax has a good mix of the roads you will see on a road test: quiet crescents, school zones, busy arterial roads and highway ramps. Lessons usually build up through them in this order:

Where do Ajax drivers take their road test?

Most Ajax drivers book their road test at a nearby centre. Popular choices for our students are Oshawa and Metro East in Scarborough, and some book Port Union. Check the full list of centres and available dates on drivetest.ca.

The best centre is the one whose roads you can practise on. Our guides to Oshawa DriveTest and Metro East DriveTest describe each area and what to expect when you check in. If you are not sure which to pick, we can help you find and book an available slot at the centre closest to you.

How many lessons will you need?

It depends on how much you have already driven. Ontario does not require a minimum number of lessons. You must hold your G1 for 12 months before your G2 road test, or 8 months if you finish a government-approved driver education course.

As a rough guide:

  1. Confident drivers who practise often with family usually need 2 to 3 hours, focused on test skills.
  2. Drivers who still hesitate at left turns or lane changes usually need 4 to 5 hours.
  3. Complete beginners are better off with 10 hours or a full BDE course.

Our guide on how many driving lessons you need before your G2 goes into more detail, including the signs that you are ready to book.

Should you take the BDE course?

A Ministry-approved BDE course lets you take your G2 road test after 8 months instead of 12, and most insurers offer a discount for new drivers who complete it. Our BDE packages combine online theory with one-on-one in-car lessons in Ajax and the surrounding area. Read whether the BDE course is worth it to decide if it fits your plans.

Do you only teach in Ajax?

No. We are based in Ajax, and our instructors also teach in Pickering, Whitby, Oshawa, Scarborough and North York, with free pick-up and drop-off within our service area. Many students start lessons near home and then spend their last lesson around the test centre they booked.

Frequently asked questions

Do you pick up students in Ajax?

Yes. Your instructor picks you up from your home, school or workplace in Ajax and drops you off at the end of the lesson, at no extra charge.

Can I practise on Highway 401 with a G1?

Only with a certified driving instructor. G1 drivers cannot use 400-series highways with a family member, but highway practice is allowed during a lesson with an instructor certified in Ontario.

Is there a DriveTest centre in Ajax?

Most Ajax drivers take their road test at a nearby centre such as Oshawa or Metro East in Scarborough. Check drivetest.ca for current locations and available dates.

Can I use your car for my road test?

Yes. Every hours plus road test bundle, and the Premium and Advanced BDE packages, include the use of our car on test day along with a warm-up lesson beforehand.

How soon can I start lessons?

As soon as you have your G1. You can book a single lesson or a bundle without enrolling in the full BDE course.
