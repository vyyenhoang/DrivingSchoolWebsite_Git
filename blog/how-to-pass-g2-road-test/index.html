How to Pass Your G2 Road Test on the First Try in Ontario

By Public Star Driving School · October 30, 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer

To pass the G2 road test on your first try, practise full stops behind the line, check mirrors and blind spots before every lane change and turn, keep a steady speed at or just under the limit, and rehearse the roads near your DriveTest centre. Most fails come from poor observation, rolling stops and unsafe lane changes, not from parking.

The G2 road test is short, usually around 20 minutes, and it checks one thing: can you drive safely on your own in normal traffic? Examiners are not looking for perfection. They are looking for good observation, smooth control and safe decisions. Here is how our instructors prepare students to pass the first time.

What the G2 road test checks

Your examiner scores you on everyday driving skills:

  • Starting, stopping and moving off from the curb
  • Left and right turns at controlled and uncontrolled intersections
  • Lane changes with mirror and blind-spot checks
  • Stop signs, traffic lights and right-of-way
  • Speed control in residential and main roads
  • One or more manoeuvres, such as a three-point turn, roadside stop or parking

Each mistake is marked on a score sheet. Small mistakes add up, and a single dangerous action ends the test.

Mistakes that cause an automatic fail

These are the errors examiners treat as dangerous. One of them is usually enough to fail. For the full list, including the car problems that cancel a test before it starts, see our G2 automatic fails guide.

  • Rolling through a stop sign. Stop fully behind the line, then creep forward if you need to see.
  • Not yielding the right of way to pedestrians, cyclists or cars that arrived first.
  • Unsafe lane changes without a shoulder check, or cutting off another car.
  • Speeding, even a little, or driving so slowly you hold up traffic.
  • Running a red or amber light you could have stopped for safely.
  • The examiner having to help, by speaking up or touching the wheel to prevent a collision.
  • Mounting the curb or hitting anything.

The most common reason students fail: observation

Most G2 fails we see are not about control. They come from not looking enough. Make these habits automatic:

  1. Before every turn: check your mirror, signal, then check your blind spot.
  2. At every intersection: look left, right and left again, even on a green light.
  3. Every 5 to 8 seconds: glance at your mirrors.
  4. Before moving off from a stop: check your blind spot on the curb side.

Move your head when you check. Examiners can only score what they can see, and eye-only checks are easy to miss.

Tips for the day of your test

  • Arrive early so you are not rushed. Bring your G1 licence and payment if you have not paid.
  • Use a car you know. Taking the test in the same car you trained in removes one source of nerves. Our road test bundles include the instructor's car and a warm-up drive first.
  • Expect a vehicle check. The examiner may ask you to show the signals, brake lights and horn work before you leave.
  • Ask if you did not hear an instruction. Asking is fine. Guessing wrong is not.
  • Do not give up after a mistake. One minor error rarely fails a test. Reset and keep driving safely.

Practise the roads near your test centre

Each DriveTest centre has its own mix of roads, school zones and tricky intersections. We train students on the areas around:

  • Metro East on Lawrence Avenue East (see our Metro East DriveTest tips)
  • Port Union in Scarborough
  • Downsview in North York
  • Oshawa

Knowing where the school zones, speed changes and busy left turns are makes the test feel familiar instead of surprising.

How many lessons do you need before the G2 test?

If you have practised with family, 3 to 5 hours with an instructor is usually enough to fix habits and learn what examiners expect. Frequently asked questions

How many mistakes are allowed on the G2 road test?

There is no fixed number published. Minor mistakes are added up on the score sheet, and one dangerous action, like a rolling stop or an unsafe lane change, can fail you on its own.

What is the most common reason people fail the G2?

Poor observation: not checking blind spots, mirrors and intersections clearly enough for the examiner to see.

Is parallel parking on the G2 road test in Ontario?

You may be asked to do a parking manoeuvre, a roadside stop or a three-point turn. Practise all of them, because you will not know in advance which ones you get.

Can I use a driving school car for the G2 test?

Yes. Every Public Star hours plus road test bundle includes the use of the instructor's car on test day, plus a warm-up lesson.
