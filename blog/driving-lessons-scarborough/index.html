Driving Lessons in Scarborough: What to Expect

By Public Star Driving School · November 27, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Driving lessons in Scarborough are one-on-one sessions with a certified instructor who picks you up from home, school or work and builds from quiet streets to busy roads like Lawrence Avenue East and Highway 401. At Public Star, a single G2 lesson is $50 + HST ($56.50) and a G lesson is $60 + HST ($67.80), and every hours plus road test bundle includes a warm-up and the use of our car at Metro East or Port Union DriveTest.

Scarborough is one of the busiest places in the GTA to learn to drive. You get wide arterial roads, heavy bus traffic, plazas on every corner and Highway 401 cutting right across the middle. That can feel like a lot at first, but it also means that once you can drive comfortably here, a road test rarely throws anything new at you.

This guide explains what a lesson with Public Star looks like in Scarborough, which local roads are worth practising on, where Scarborough drivers usually take their road test, and what lessons cost.

What happens in your first driving lesson in Scarborough?

Your instructor picks you up from your home, school or workplace and starts somewhere calm, usually a residential street away from the main roads. The first lesson is about finding out where you are, not about testing you.

In that first hour, expect to:

  • Set up the car properly: seat, mirrors and steering wheel, before you move.
  • Show what you can already do: smooth starts and stops, turns, and keeping your lane.
  • Get clear feedback: what you already do well and what needs work.
  • Agree on a plan: roughly how many lessons you need and what each one will cover.

If you have never driven before, you stay on quiet streets until steering and braking feel natural. If you already practise with family, your instructor will move faster to the things that cost marks on a road test, such as observation at intersections and lane changes.

What do you need before you can book a lesson?

You need a G1 licence, which you get after passing the vision test and the written knowledge test at a DriveTest centre. Ontario's G1 rules say you must:

  • Keep a zero blood alcohol level.
  • Make sure every passenger wears a working seatbelt.
  • Not drive between midnight and 5 a.m.
  • Not drive on 400-series highways or high-speed expressways such as the 401, QEW or Gardiner.
  • Drive with a fully licensed driver who has at least four years of driving experience.

There is one important exception: when you drive with a driving instructor certified in Ontario, you can drive on any road. That matters in Scarborough, because the 401 is never far away. A lesson is the only way a G1 driver can get real highway practice before the G2 stage.

For the full list of what changes after you pass, see our guide to G2 licence rules in Ontario.

Where do Scarborough drivers take their road test?

Scarborough has two DriveTest centres close by, and most of our Scarborough students book one of them:

  • Metro East, on Lawrence Avenue East. Read our Metro East DriveTest tips for what to expect when you check in and the type of roads around the centre.
  • Port Union, on Rylander Boulevard in east Scarborough. Our Port Union DriveTest guide explains the area and how to prepare.

Some students also book Oshawa when it has earlier dates. Check current locations, hours and available dates on drivetest.ca. The best centre is usually the one whose roads you can practise on, and we can help you find an open slot.

How many lessons will you need?

Ontario does not set a minimum number of lessons. What it does set is a waiting period: you can take your G2 road test after 12 months with a G1, or after eight months if you finish a government-approved driver education course. After you pass, you can take the G road test after 12 months with a G2.

As a rough guide to lessons:

  1. Confident drivers who already practise often usually need 2 to 3 hours focused on test skills.
  2. Drivers who still hesitate at left turns, lane changes or busy intersections usually need 4 to 5 hours.
  3. Complete beginners do best with 10 hours or a full BDE course.

Our guide on how many driving lessons you need before your G2 goes into more detail, including the signs you are ready to book.

Should you take the BDE course?

A government-approved BDE course lets you take your G2 road test after eight months instead of 12, and many insurers offer a discount to new drivers who complete one. Our BDE packages combine online theory with one-on-one in-car lessons in Scarborough and the surrounding area. Read whether the BDE course is worth it to decide if it fits your plans.

Do you only teach in Scarborough?

No. Public Star is based in Ajax, and our instructors teach across Scarborough as well as Ajax, Pickering, Whitby, Oshawa and North York, with free pick-up and drop-off within our service area. Many students start near home and then spend their last lesson around the test centre they booked.

Frequently asked questions

Do you pick up students in Scarborough?

Yes. Your instructor picks you up from your home, school or workplace in Scarborough and drops you off at the end of the lesson, at no extra charge.

Can I drive on Highway 401 with a G1?

Only with a driving instructor certified in Ontario. With a family member, G1 drivers cannot use 400-series highways or high-speed expressways, but an instructor can take you on any road.

Which DriveTest centre is closest to Scarborough?

Metro East on Lawrence Avenue East and Port Union in east Scarborough are the two closest. Check drivetest.ca for current hours and available dates.

Can I use your car for my road test in Scarborough?

Yes. Every hours plus road test bundle, and the Premium and Advanced BDE packages, include the use of our car on test day with a warm-up lesson beforehand.

How soon can I start lessons?

As soon as you have your G1. You can book a single lesson or a bundle without signing up for the full BDE course.
