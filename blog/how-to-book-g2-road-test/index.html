How to Book a G2 Road Test at DriveTest: Step by Step

By Public Star Driving School · November 21, 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer

Book your G2 road test online at drivetest.ca with an email address, your driver's licence number and expiry date, and a VISA, MasterCard, VISA Debit or Mastercard Debit card. You can book once you have held your G1 for 12 months, or 8 months if your BDE results are in the MTO system. The G2 road test fee is $53.75 (already prepaid if you bought the $159.75 G1 package), and you must cancel or reschedule at least 48 hours ahead or you lose the fee.

Booking your G2 road test turns months of practice into a real date. The online system is quick, but a few details trip people up: email verification, accepted cards and the 48 hour rule for changes. This guide follows DriveTest's own booking instructions.

When can you book your G2 road test?

You can book once you are eligible, which depends on how long you have held your G1:

Your situationEarliest G2 road test
Standard G1After 12 months on your G1
Completed a government-approved BDE courseAfter 8 months on your G1

The 8 month option only works after your driving school has entered your course results into the Ministry of Transportation (MTO) system. DriveTest says that if the results are not entered, the booking calendar will show your first available date as 12 months from when you applied. If that happens after a BDE course, contact your course provider and ask them to confirm your results are in. Our guide to whether the BDE course is worth it explains what the course includes, and the G1 to G licence timeline shows how the waiting periods fit together.

What you need before you start

Gather these before you open the booking page:

  • An email address. DriveTest may not support bookings for different licences using the same email, so each driver should use their own.
  • Your driver's licence number and expiry date. Use the expiry date from your permanent licence card, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
  • A valid card. Online booking accepts VISA, MasterCard, VISA Debit and Mastercard Debit only. Regular Interac debit does not work online.
  • An up to date browser. DriveTest recommends recent versions of Chrome, Firefox, Microsoft Edge or Safari. Internet Explorer does not work.

How to book a G2 road test online, step by step

DriveTest says booking normally takes about 5 minutes, and you have 45 minutes in total to finish a session.

  1. Go to drivetest.ca and click "Book a Road Test." At busy times you may be placed in a virtual waiting room. Keep that window open, or you lose your place in the queue.
  2. Enter your email, licence number and expiry date. If it is your first booking, or you changed your email, DriveTest sends a verification email from noreply@drivetest.ca. Open it and click "Verify Email" in the same browser, within the time allowed. Check your junk folder if it does not arrive.
  3. Choose "Book a New Road Test" and pick Class G2.
  4. Pick a DriveTest location. A map and list show every centre that offers the G2 test. You can choose any centre, not only the closest one.
  5. Pick a date and time. White boxes on the calendar have openings, grey boxes do not. Select a time slot and continue.
  6. Complete the security check and pay. If your fee is already on file, you just agree to the terms and book. If not, review the summary, then enter your card details and click "Pay & Book Now."
  7. Watch for your confirmation. You will see "Your road test is booked!" and receive confirmation emails for the payment and the appointment.

DriveTest says a printed confirmation is not required at check in, since you can show the details on your phone. Our checklist of what to bring to your road test covers everything else for the day.

What does the G2 road test cost?

These are DriveTest's current fees, which include taxes:

DriveTest feeAmount
Class G1 licence package (knowledge test, G2 road test and five-year licence)$159.75
Class G2 road test$53.75
Class G road test$91.25

If you paid for the G1 licence package, your first G2 road test is already included, so the booking system may show a payment on file and skip the card step. If you need to retake the test, you pay the $53.75 G2 road test fee when you book again. Confirm current amounts on the DriveTest fee list.

Other ways to book: phone or in person

You do not have to book online. DriveTest says appointment availability is the same across every channel, so no method gets you an earlier date. You can:

  • Call the automated booking line at 1-888-570-6110 or 647-776-0331, and press 0 from the main menu to reach a live agent during business hours.
  • Visit any DriveTest Centre and book at the counter. Centres also accept cash, regular debit and other payment types that the website does not.

Some people must book by phone or in person, including anyone whose licence has expired or is suspended, anyone without an email address, and anyone who took a road test in the last 24 hours.

How to reschedule or cancel your G2 road test

Log in the same way you booked and open your Dashboard. It lists every test you have booked, whether online, by phone or at a centre. From there you can reschedule or click "Cancel Test."

The key rule: cancel or reschedule at least 48 hours before your appointment. DriveTest says you lose your prepaid road test fee if you cancel within 48 hours. Arriving late or not showing up also costs you the fee.

A few more limits to know:

  • You can only hold one appointment at a time online.
  • If you have cancelled or rescheduled 3 or more road tests in 6 months, you must call DriveTest instead of using the website.
  • If your licence has expired, you cannot cancel or change appointments online.

How to find an earlier road test date

DriveTest says its full time centres aim to have road tests available within 42 days, and it posts appointments up to six months ahead. Openings also appear through the month as other people cancel. To get an earlier date:

  • Check several centres. Availability differs from one location to another. Around our area, compare the Port Union, Metro East and Oshawa centres.
  • Log in and check again. New times show up as others cancel or reschedule.
  • Ask about standby. Road tests are by appointment only, but you can visit a DriveTest Centre and ask to be added to its standby list. Standby is first come, first served, with no guarantee a spot opens.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book my G2 road test before my 12 months on a G1 are up?

The booking calendar will not show dates before you are eligible. With a government-approved BDE course entered in the MTO system, you can test after 8 months instead of 12.

What cards does DriveTest accept for online booking?

VISA, MasterCard, VISA Debit and Mastercard Debit only. To pay with cash or regular debit, book at a DriveTest Centre.

Can I choose which DriveTest centre I take my G2 test at?

Yes. During booking you pick any centre that offers the G2 test, so you can compare dates at several locations.

What happens if I cancel less than 48 hours before my test?

You lose your prepaid road test fee. Cancel or reschedule at least 48 hours ahead to keep it.
