Port Union DriveTest: Road Test Guide for the East Scarborough Centre

By Public Star Driving School · November 19, 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer

Port Union DriveTest is at The Village of Abbey Lane Shopping Centre, 91 Rylander Blvd, Unit 109A, Toronto, and it runs G2 and G road tests Monday to Saturday from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The G2 road test costs $53.75 and the G road test costs $91.25, and Ontario asks you to arrive at least 30 minutes before your appointment. Bring a car in good working order, your glasses or contacts if you need them, and plan a ride home if you are testing on a G1.

Port Union is the DriveTest centre in the east end of Scarborough, close to the Pickering border. Many of our students from Scarborough, Pickering and Ajax book it because it is near home or has an earlier opening than other centres. This guide covers the practical side: where it is, when road tests run, what they cost, what to bring and how to prepare.

Where is the Port Union DriveTest centre?

DriveTest lists the centre as Toronto Port Union at:

The Village of Abbey Lane Shopping Centre, 91 Rylander Blvd, Unit 109A, Toronto, M1B 5M5

It is inside a shopping plaza, so you park in the plaza lot and walk in to check in. Plazas get busy on weekends, so leave a few extra minutes to find a spot. If you are driving in from Pickering, Ajax or Whitby, check traffic before you leave so you are not rushing on test day.

Port Union hours and road tests offered

According to DriveTest's alphabetical centre list, Port Union offers road tests for Class G2 and Class G only. If you need a motorcycle or commercial road test, you will have to book a different centre.

How much does a road test at Port Union cost?

Road test fees are set by the Government of Ontario and are the same at every DriveTest centre. These are DriveTest's current fees, taxes included:

Road testDriveTest fee
Class G2 road test$53.75
Class G road test$91.25

Your first G2 road test is already included in the $159.75 G1 licence package you paid when you applied. You pay again for the G road test and for any retest. DriveTest accepts cash, Visa, Mastercard, debit and a few other methods listed on its fees page. Ontario's handbook also reminds drivers that the ministry and DriveTest do not charge fees to book road tests, so be careful of sites that do.

What to bring to your Port Union road test

Ontario's handbook gives the same checklist for the G2 and G road tests:

  • A vehicle in good working order. It must be fit, in proper working condition, plated and insured.
  • Money for test fees, if you have not prepaid.
  • Glasses or contact lenses if you need them to drive.
  • Your arrival plan. Arrive at least 30 minutes before your road test appointment.

For the G test, you also need highway driving experience on roads with speed limits of at least 80 km/h, and you complete a Declaration of Highway Driving Experience form before the test. Our G road test highway tips cover how to build that experience.

Rules for test day

These rules come from Ontario's handbook and apply at every centre, Port Union included:

  1. No passengers. Only you and the examiner are in the car. Driving instructors, friends, relatives and translators cannot come along, and no pets either.
  2. No driving aids. Automatic parallel parking, lane monitoring, cruise control and back cameras may not be used. Practise with mirrors and shoulder checks.
  3. The examiner cannot coach you. Ask any questions before you start driving.
  4. Plan your ride home. If you are a G1 driver, an accompanying driver must come with you to the centre. If you are a G2 driver, be ready to get home another way in case you do not pass.
  5. Cancel early. If you cancel or reschedule with less than 48 hours notice, or miss the test, your prepaid fee is not refunded.
  6. Bring a ready car. If the car does not meet ministry standards, the test is declared out-of-order and you lose 50% of the fee.

For a walk-through of check-in and the vehicle check, our Metro East DriveTest guide explains the steps, which are the same at every centre.

How to prepare for driving around Port Union

The centre sits in a mostly residential part of east Scarborough, with quieter neighbourhood streets close to busier main roads. That mix is good news: you can practise most of what the test asks for nearby. Focus on:

  • Residential streets: full stops at stop signs, lower speed limits, and watching for pedestrians and children near schools and parks.
  • Busier main roads: lane changes with a mirror and shoulder check every time, and steady speed control.
  • Intersections: left turns on a green light, waiting in the right spot and turning into the correct lane.
  • Parking and reversing: the handbook lists parallel parking and reversing as skills you will be tested on. See our parallel parking guide and our three-point turn and roadside stop guide.

There is no official ranking of "easy" DriveTest centres, and we will not invent one. Choose the centre whose roads you can practise on most. Students in east Scarborough and Pickering often find Port Union convenient, while others book Metro East or Oshawa.

If you do not pass

The examiner gives you a full report at the end of the test and explains any mistakes. If you do not pass, Ontario says you must wait at least 10 days before your next test. Use that time to work on the exact points on your report. Our guide to G2 road test automatic fails explains the mistakes that end a test early.

Frequently asked questions

What road tests does Port Union DriveTest offer?

DriveTest lists Port Union for Class G2 and Class G road tests. Motorcycle and commercial road tests are not offered there.

What are the Port Union DriveTest road test hours?

Road test appointments run Monday to Saturday from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Inside services run Monday to Friday 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. and Saturday 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.

How early should I arrive for my road test at Port Union?

Ontario's road test checklist says to arrive at least 30 minutes before your appointment.

Can my driving instructor ride with me during the test?

No. Only you and the examiner can be in the car. Your instructor can drive you there, warm you up and wait for you.

Is Port Union easier than other DriveTest centres?

There is no official pass rate ranking by centre. The best choice is a centre whose roads you can practise on before your test.
