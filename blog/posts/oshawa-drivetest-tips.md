Oshawa is the main DriveTest centre for drivers in Durham Region. Many of our students from Oshawa, Whitby, Ajax and Pickering book their G2 or G test there because it is close to home and they can practise on the same kinds of roads. This guide covers the practical side: where the centre is, when road tests run, what they cost, what happens after you check in, and how to get ready for driving in that part of Oshawa.

## Where is the Oshawa DriveTest centre?

DriveTest lists the centre as **Oshawa** at:

**Midtown Mall, 200 John St W, Oshawa, L1J 2B4**

The centre is inside a mall, so you park in the lot and walk in to check in. Leave extra time if you are driving in from Whitby, Ajax or Pickering. Highway 401 and the main east to west roads can back up at rush hour, and arriving late and rushed is a bad way to start a road test.

DriveTest's centre list shows Oshawa as a full-time centre that offers a wide range of road tests, including G2 and G for cars, M2 and M for motorcycles, and commercial classes.

## What happens on test day in Oshawa

The process is the same at every DriveTest centre. Knowing it ahead of time takes a lot of the nerves away.

1. **Arrive at least 30 minutes early.** DriveTest asks for this at every centre.
2. **Park and go inside to check in.** Bring your licence and be ready to confirm your booking.
3. **Go back to your car and wait.** The examiner comes to you in the parking lot.
4. **Vehicle check.** Before you drive, the examiner checks that the car is safe. Lights, signals, horn, brakes, tires and the windshield all matter.
5. **The drive.** DriveTest says the G2 test usually takes about 20 minutes.
6. **Results.** The examiner gives you a short summary at the end along with your scoresheet.

A few rules catch people out:

- **No passengers.** Your instructor, friends and family cannot ride along. Only you and the examiner are in the car.
- **No recording.** Phones and dashboard cameras must be off during the test.
- **No driving aids.** Features such as automatic parking, lane keeping, cruise control and back cameras may not be used during the test. Practise parking with your mirrors and shoulder checks.
- **Plan your ride home.** If you take your G2 test on a G1, you cannot drive home alone if you do not pass, so bring a fully licensed driver or arrange another ride.

## How to prepare for the roads around Oshawa DriveTest

DriveTest does not publish its test routes, and examiners can take you anywhere nearby. Instead of memorising one route, get comfortable with the kinds of roads you will meet around downtown Oshawa:

- **Downtown streets.** Expect traffic lights close together, buses, pedestrians and cars pulling in and out of parking spots. Some downtown streets are one-way, so read every sign and stay in the correct lane before you turn.
- **Busy arterial roads.** Multi-lane roads in Oshawa carry heavy traffic and lots of turning cars. Do a mirror check, signal and shoulder check for every lane change.
- **Left turns at busy lights.** Move into the intersection when it is safe, wait for a real gap, and turn into the correct lane. Never rush a left turn because someone behind you is impatient.
- **Residential streets.** Quiet side streets mean stop signs, school zones, parked cars and people walking. Stop fully behind the line, look both ways, and watch your speed.
- **Leaving and entering the mall lot.** Your test starts and ends in a parking lot. Drive slowly, watch for pedestrians, and signal when you pull onto the road.
- **Parking and reversing.** The G2 test checks parking and reversing, so practise until it feels routine.

If you are taking the G test, add highway practice on Highway 401 and Highway 407 ramps if you can. ## Should you take your test in Oshawa or somewhere else?

There is no official ranking of "easy" DriveTest centres, and we will not make one up. Every centre uses the same scoring standards. The better question is which centre's roads you can practise on the most. If you live in Oshawa, Whitby or Courtice, Oshawa is usually the most convenient choice. Students in Scarborough often prefer [Metro East DriveTest](/blog/metro-east-drivetest-tips/), and some in Pickering and Ajax book Port Union. Pick the centre near where you can get real practice, then book early, because test dates can fill up.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the address of Oshawa DriveTest?

Midtown Mall, 200 John St W, Oshawa, L1J 2B4, according to DriveTest's centre list.

### Does Oshawa DriveTest do G road tests?

Yes. DriveTest lists Oshawa as offering G2 and G road tests, plus motorcycle and commercial classes.

### What time are road tests at Oshawa DriveTest?

DriveTest lists road test appointments Monday to Saturday from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Check the service disruptions page on the day of your test.

### Can my driving instructor ride with me during the test?

No. Only you and the examiner can be in the car. Your instructor can drive you there, warm you up, and wait for you.

### Is Oshawa DriveTest harder than other centres?

There is no official data showing one centre is harder than another. What matters most is practising on roads like the ones around the centre you choose.
