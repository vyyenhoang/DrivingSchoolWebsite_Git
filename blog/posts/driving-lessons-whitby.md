## What does a driving lesson in Whitby look like?

Every lesson is one-on-one, so the full hour is yours. Your instructor picks you up from your home, school or workplace in Whitby and plans the lesson around what you need next.

Your first lesson usually includes:

- **Getting set up:** seat, mirrors and steering wheel position before you move.
- **A quick assessment:** starts, stops, turns and lane position on quiet streets.
- **Honest feedback:** what you already do well and which habits would cost marks on a road test.
- **A plan:** a realistic estimate of how many lessons you need and what each one will cover.

Each lesson after that picks up where the last one ended, with extra time on the skills family practice often misses, such as full stops and shoulder checks.

## What licence do you need to start lessons in Whitby?

You need a **G1 licence**, which you get by passing a vision test and the written knowledge test at a DriveTest centre. Once you have it, you can start lessons with a certified driving instructor right away.

While you hold a G1 in Ontario, you must:

- Keep a zero blood alcohol level.
- Not drive between midnight and 5 a.m.
- Not drive on 400-series highways or high-speed expressways such as the 401.
- Always drive with a fully licensed driver who has at least four years of driving experience.

There is one important exception. When you drive with a driving instructor certified in Ontario, you can drive on any road. For Whitby drivers, that matters: Highway 401 runs along the south end of town and Highway 407 crosses the north, and a lesson is the only way to practise on them while you hold a G1.

## Local roads worth practising on in Whitby

Whitby has quiet subdivisions, a busy downtown, wide arterial roads and highway access, all within a short drive. Lessons usually move through them in stages:

| Stage | Type of road | What you practise |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beginner | Residential streets in neighbourhoods like Brooklin and Williamsburg | Steering control, full stops, speed control, scanning for pedestrians |
| Building up | Main roads such as Thickson Road, Garden Street and Anderson Street | Left turns at lights, lane changes, following distance |

## How many lessons will you need?

Ontario does not set a minimum number of lessons. It does set waiting periods: you must hold your G1 for 12 months before your G2 road test, or 8 months if you finish a government-approved driver education course. After that, you hold your G2 for 12 months before you can take the G road test.

As a rough guide for Whitby students:

1. **Confident drivers** who practise often with family usually need 2 to 3 hours of test-focused lessons.
2. **Drivers who still hesitate** at left turns, lane changes or busy intersections usually need 4 to 5 hours.
3. **Complete beginners** do best with 10 hours or a full BDE course.
