FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to complete the full BDE course to take lessons?

No. If you already have a G1 or G2, you can book individual lessons or an hours-plus-road-test bundle without enrolling in the full course.

What does the BDE certificate get me?

Graduating from a Ministry-approved BDE course lets you take your G2 road test after 8 months instead of 12, and most insurers offer a discount for new drivers who complete it.

Can I use your car for my road test?

Yes. Every hours-plus-road-test bundle and the Premium and Advanced BDE packages include the use of the instructor's car on test day, along with a warm-up lesson beforehand.

How do pick-up and drop-off work?

Your instructor picks you up from your home, school or workplace within our service area and drops you off at the end of the lesson, at no extra charge.

How do I pay?

No payment is required to request a booking. We'll confirm your schedule first and arrange payment by e-transfer or in person before your first lesson.

Can I book my DriveTest appointment through you?

Yes. We'll help you find and book an available road test slot at the DriveTest centre closest to you.
